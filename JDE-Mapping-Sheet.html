JDE Mapping Sheet

Turn a third-party JSON payload into one row per field, then map each field to a JD Edwards table and alias. Everything runs in your browser.

Payload Example payload

Every field in the payload becomes a row. Fill in the JDE table, alias, description and any transformation logic. Typing an alias that exists in your Excel data fills in the table and description. Click a heading to sort, type under it to filter, and drag the edge of a heading to resize (double-click the edge to reset), and drag the ⋮⋮ grip to move a column. Sort by # to get back to the original order.

Upload your Excel sheet and it appears here with all of its columns, plus a JSON path column. Drag a field from the payload onto any row and the full path is filled in. On a touch screen, tap a payload field, then tap the row.
